VibeWave Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Terms of Service · Delete your account

VibeWave (package com.vibewave.musicremix) is a music app made by an independent developer in Pakistan. It lets you add effects to songs, make videos, write and create songs with AI, and share songs on Explore. This policy explains what data we use, why, who we share it with and how you can delete it.

Short version: we only use your data to run the app. We never sell your personal data. You can use most of the app without an account, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Data we collect and why

Account (only if you sign in with Google)

  • Your name, email address and a Google account ID, received through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.
  • Used to keep your AI songs, credits, purchases, likes and published songs linked to you on every phone.

AI Studio

  • The messages and ideas you type, the language and voice you choose, and the lyrics, titles and audio the AI creates for you.
  • Sent to our server and to AI providers (see section 3) only to create your lyrics and songs.
  • Your AI songs and lyrics are saved on our server so you can open them again in "My AI songs". Your chat messages are saved only on your phone.
  • We keep short text fingerprints of lyrics to stop two users getting the same song and to block copied lyrics.

Explore

  • If you publish a song: the audio, title, lyrics (only if you choose to show them), cover picture and the name you choose to show. This is public and anyone using VibeWave can see and hear it.
  • Likes and reports you make, with the reason you choose.
  • Play counts. To count one play per person we use a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your account ID or a random app ID. We cannot turn it back into your identity.

Songs, remixes and videos

  • To show your music, the app reads audio files on your phone with your permission. Most effects and all video editing happen on your phone.
  • Some effects and lyric detection send the song to our server for processing. These files are deleted automatically within 2 hours. Lyric detection uses a speech-to-text provider (see section 3).
  • Photos and videos you pick in Video Studio stay on your phone.

Purchases

  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.
  • We receive the product, order ID, purchase token and dates, to give you Premium Plus or Song Packs and to stop fraud.

App and device data

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): device model, Android version and what the app was doing when it crashed, so we can fix bugs.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): which screens and features are used, in a way that does not show your name or email.
  • App Check (Firebase): confirms requests come from the real VibeWave app, to stop abuse.
  • Your IP address is used for a short time to limit spam and attacks.

Ads

  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage to show and measure ads.
  • In the EU, UK and similar regions we ask for your consent first. You can reset or turn off ad personalization in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Premium users do not see ads.

Google Drive backup (optional)

  • If you choose "Backup to Google Drive", your remixes (audio files) and their details, like name, effect and favourites, are stored in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. We cannot see your other Drive files.

2. Permissions

  • Music and audio / storage: to list and play your songs and save remixes.
  • Photos and videos: we use Android's photo picker, so the app only gets the items you pick for videos. No photo permission is needed.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls and when your remix or video is ready.
  • Internet: for AI features, Explore, ads and purchases.
  • Foreground service (media playback): so music keeps playing when the app is in the background.

4. How long we keep data

  • Account, AI songs, likes and published songs: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Files sent for effects or lyric detection: deleted within 2 hours.
  • Purchase records: up to 5 years, because tax and Google Play billing rules need them.
  • Reports about songs: kept without your name so moderation stays fair.
  • Crash and analytics data: kept by Firebase according to Google's retention settings (up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports).

5. Delete your data

  • In the app: Profile › tap your account › Delete account. This deletes your account and its data straight away.
  • On the web: imran056-lofi-remix-api.hf.space/delete-account. We delete it within 30 days.
  • You can also delete single AI songs in "My AI songs" and remove your songs from Explore at any time.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our storage is private and only reachable through our server. Admin access is protected. No system is 100% safe, but we work to protect your data and fix problems quickly.

7. Children

VibeWave is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. If you live in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the app.
