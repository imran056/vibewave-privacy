Privacy Policy

VibeWave · Last updated: 26 August 2026

This policy explains what VibeWave does with your information. We have tried to keep it short and specific, because a policy you cannot read is not much use to anyone.

The short version: VibeWave does not require an account, does not collect your name or email, and does not sell anything about you. Songs you process are sent to our server, used to create your remix, and deleted within two hours.

What we collect

Audio you choose to process. When you apply an effect, add a beat, or ask for subtitles, the audio file is uploaded to our processing server. It is used only to produce the result you asked for.

Photos you choose. Images you pick for a video stay on your device. They are never uploaded.

Advertising identifier. If you use the free version, Google AdMob may access your device's advertising ID to show ads. Premium users see no ads.

Basic crash and usage data. Google Play collects standard crash reports so we can fix problems.

What we do NOT collect

  • No name, email address, or phone number
  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No contacts, messages, or call logs
  • No location
  • No browsing history
  • No photos or files other than the audio you explicitly choose

How long we keep your audio

Uploaded audio and generated files are deleted from our server automatically, within two hours of processing. We do not keep copies, and we do not listen to, analyse, or share the music you upload.

The finished remix is saved on your device, in the Music/VibeWave folder. It stays there even if you uninstall the app, and you can delete it at any time with any file manager.

Third-party services

Groq — used to generate subtitles from audio. When you request subtitles, the audio is passed through our server to Groq's transcription service. See Groq's privacy policy.

Google AdMob — shows ads in the free version. See how Google uses data from apps.

Google Play Services — used for app distribution, crash reporting, and billing.

Backup

Your settings and your list of remixes may be backed up to your own Google account, using Android's standard backup feature. This is controlled by your device settings, not by us, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Settings → Google → Backup.

Children

VibeWave is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete your remixes — from inside the app, or from the Music/VibeWave folder on your device.
  • Reset your advertising ID — Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Remove everything — uninstalling the app removes all app data. Files in Music/VibeWave remain unless you delete them yourself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top will be updated and the change will be described here.
